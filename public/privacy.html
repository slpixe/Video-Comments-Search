Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

About This App

Slpixe Video Comment Search is a web application that lets you search through comments on YouTube videos. It is hosted at video-comment-search.slpixe.com.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data ourselves. We do not use analytics, cookies, or tracking of any kind.

Google OAuth & YouTube API

This application uses the YouTube Data API v3 and Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate you and retrieve public YouTube comment data on your behalf. When you log in with Google, an OAuth access token is issued by Google and stored only in your browser's sessionStorage for the duration of your session. It is never sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Your use of this application is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can revoke this application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

Use and Retention of Google Data

We use the access token only to authorize YouTube API requests for the comments and replies you choose to view. Comment data is held in browser memory while displayed. It is cleared when you log out, replace the search, or close the page. The saved token is removed when it expires or you log out; your browser also clears session storage when its browsing session ends. We do not operate a server-side account database or retain a copy of your Google data.

We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Slpixe Video Comment Search's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Services

All comment data displayed is fetched directly from the YouTube Data API v3 and is publicly available. We do not share your data with any third parties.
