Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

About This App

Slpixe Video Comment Search is a web application that lets you search through comments on YouTube videos. It is hosted at video-comment-search.slpixe.com.

Data We Collect

We do not operate an account database, analytics or tracking. Login state is kept in your browser as described below. Google handles sign-in and API requests under its own privacy policy.

Google OAuth & YouTube API

Google login requests only basic sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile), not access to your YouTube account. Google issues a login access token, which is kept in browser sessionStorage until it expires or you log out. We do not fetch or store your name, email address or profile details in an account database.

Public comments and replies are fetched directly from the YouTube Data API v3 using the app owner's website-restricted API key. Your Google token is not sent with these requests. Google receives the submitted video ID, search term and normal request information. Searches use the app project's shared API quota; signing in does not provide your own API key.

Your use of this application is subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can revoke this application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

Use and Retention of Google Data

The login token maintains the sign-in session in your browser and is not used to read or change your YouTube account. Comment data is held in browser memory while displayed. It is cleared when you log out, replace the search, or close the page. The saved token is removed when it expires or you log out; your browser also clears session storage when its browsing session ends. We do not operate a server-side account database or retain a copy of your Google data.

We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Slpixe Video Comment Search's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Services

All comment data displayed is fetched directly from the YouTube Data API v3 and is publicly available. We do not send your login token to the YouTube comment endpoints or share it with other services.
